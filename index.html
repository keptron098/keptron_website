    About Keptron Electronics

    Proudly manufacturing 100% in India since 1991. Our UPS systems are designed, built, and tested at our manufacturing facility in Delhi. With offices in Kerala, Delhi, and Mumbai — supporting India's industrial growth and the self-reliance mission.

    As a trusted Indian manufacturer, we contribute to the Make in India initiative by providing world-class power protection solutions that reduce reliance on imported equipment while supporting local employment and skill development.

    We are committed to environmental responsibility. Our UPS systems are built using components sourced from recycled and eco-friendly materials, reducing our carbon footprint while delivering world-class power protection.

    Our energy-efficient designs minimise power loss and extend the life of connected equipment, contributing to a more sustainable future.

    Serving customers worldwide with reliable power solutions, certified products (ISO, CE, BIS), and exceptional after-sales support backed by over three decades of expertise.

    Our clientele includes government organisations, banks, defence, telecom, energy, and logistics sectors across India and beyond.

    Our products comply with internationally recognised quality and safety standards:

    • ISO Certified — Quality management systems compliant with ISO standards.
    • CE Certified — Meets European Conformity standards for safety, health, and environmental protection.
    • BIS Certified — Bureau of Indian Standards approval for reliability and performance.
