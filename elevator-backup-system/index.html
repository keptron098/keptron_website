Elevator Backup System for Buildings

How to keep lifts running safely during power failures

Why Elevators Need Backup Power

Power cuts can trap passengers and halt access in multi-storey buildings. A backup system keeps lifts operating, or brings them to a safe floor, until mains power returns.
