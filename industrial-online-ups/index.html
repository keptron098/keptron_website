Keptron Industrial Online UPS

Keptron Electronics has manufactured Industrial Online UPS systems in India since 1991. An online (double-conversion) UPS continuously supplies clean, regulated power to the connected load, so critical equipment is protected from outages, voltage fluctuations and disturbances on the mains.

Our range serves industries, OEMs, builders, dealers, distributors and institutional customers, and is ISO, CE and BIS certified.
