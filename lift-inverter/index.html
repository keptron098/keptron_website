Reliable Lift Inverter for Elevator Backup During Power Failure

Keptron Lift Inverters for elevator companies, OEMs, builders, hospitals and commercial buildings

What is a Lift Inverter?

A lift inverter is a power backup system designed for elevators. When mains power fails, it supplies the lift from a battery bank so the elevator can keep operating or bring passengers safely to the nearest floor, depending on the configuration.

How Does It Work During Power Failure?

The system senses the mains failure and switches the lift supply to the inverter, which runs from batteries. When mains power returns, the lift is transferred back and the batteries are recharged.

How Does It Support Elevator Operation?

  • Reduces the risk of passengers being stranded between floors
  • Keeps lifts available in apartments, hospitals and commercial buildings
  • Helps buildings meet occupant safety and convenience expectations

Available Models and Specifications

Models and capacities are selected based on lift motor rating, load and the backup you need. Contact us with your lift details for a recommendation and quotation.

Compatible Lift Configurations

Keptron lift inverters are sized to the lift controller/drive and motor. Share your lift type (geared or gearless), motor rating and controller details, and we will confirm compatibility before supply.

Backup and Rescue Functionality

Depending on the model and lift controller, the inverter can power the lift to continue normal operation or to move the car to the nearest floor and open the doors. Tell us which you need.

Applications

  • Residential apartments and housing societies
  • Commercial buildings, offices and malls
  • Hospitals and healthcare facilities
  • Hotels, schools and institutional buildings

Installation Information

The inverter is installed between the mains supply and the lift panel, with a battery bank sized for the required backup. Installation should be done by qualified personnel; our team can advise on wiring, battery placement and commissioning.
