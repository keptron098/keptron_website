About Keptron Electronics

Who We Are

Established in 1991, Keptron Electronics is a manufacturer, exporter and supplier of Industrial Online UPS systems and Lift Inverters. Our single phase range covers 1 kVA to 20 kVA and our three phase range extends up to 300 kVA.

Make in India

We manufacture in India, with offices in Delhi, Kerala and Mumbai. Our products are designed, built and tested at our facility in Delhi.

Certifications and Standards

  • ISO Certified - quality management systems
  • CE Certified - European conformity for safety, health and environmental protection
  • BIS Certified - Bureau of Indian Standards approval

Our Customers

Our clientele includes government organisations, banks, defence, telecom, energy and logistics sectors across India and beyond. See customer testimonials and our product range.
